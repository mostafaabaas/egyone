03 · Egypt market guides

Know the market before you spend in it

Three practical guides from daily work with brands and creators in Egypt — the key points are complete here.

Choosing an agency

How to choose a marketing agency in Egypt: the questions and the red flags

The decision does not come from the prettiest pitch deck — it comes from specific questions with their answers in writing before you sign with anyone.

  • Ask who actually does the work: a fixed in-house team, or freelancers assembled per project?
  • Content and raw-footage rights: whose are they, where, and for how long?
  • Ask for a real sample report: what will you see, and how often?
  • Red flag: guaranteed-number promises, or a price with no written scope

UGC in Egypt

The UGC that actually works in the Egyptian market

The video that sells here speaks natural Egyptian, answers the value question, and ends with a clear step on WhatsApp.

  • A translated script or heavy formal Arabic reads instantly as a foreign ad
  • A creator who resembles your customer beats a star passing over many products
  • The "how much?" question is part of the content — answer it with value, not evasion
  • Seasons change everything: Ramadan, summer, back-to-school

Market geography

Cairo, Alexandria and the Delta: not one market with one voice

The same product sells three different ways depending on the region — and the creators, locations and seasonal calendars change with it.

  • Greater Cairo: speed and variety — and even inside it, every district is a different audience
  • Alexandria: a clear local pride, and a summer that changes the whole city's rhythm
  • The Delta: trust travels person to person — practical proof wins
  • Decide who and where first, then build the content on the answer

04 · FAQ

What Egyptian brands ask first

Straight answers before a first project — coverage, starting, rights and prices.

Is egy.one a separate agency?

No. egy.one is a specialised gateway for Egyptian brands within one network: a single entry point organised around the Egyptian market — its cities, its creators and its seasons — with every service delivered by one Egyptian team from the same base.

Do you work outside Cairo?

Yes. On-the-ground execution covers Cairo, Giza, 6th of October City, New Cairo, Obour, Maadi, Nasr City, Alexandria, Mansoura and Tanta, and digital campaigns reach every governorate in Egypt.

How do I find Egyptian creators and influencers who fit my brand?

Two ways: a direct shortlist from the team, matched to your category, city and audience and approved by you before any execution — or search yourself in the creator discovery tool at explorinfluencer.com, connected to the network's creator database.

Do I have to start with a big campaign?

No. Most brands start small: one UGC batch or one month of social media management — and we scale based on results and reports, not promises.

Who owns the content that gets produced?

Usage rights are written into the agreement from the start: where you can use the content and for how long, and the position on raw footage — with no surprises after delivery.

Are prices published?

There are no published prices. After the free consultation you receive a written quote with a clear scope: the services, the deliverables and the timeline — priced on the actual size of the execution.
